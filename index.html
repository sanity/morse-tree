Morse Tree

 
Tap the key to start a letter

Tap the key for a dot, hold it for a dash. Starting from the top, every dot takes you down the left branch and every dash down the right, and the lights follow your path. To start a new letter, just pause: when the amber bar runs out, the letter you landed on is typed and your next tap starts again from the top. Keep pausing until the blue bar also runs out to add a space between words (or press space). Press next letter (Enter) to end a letter immediately. With auto speed on, the timing learns from your keying, so it keeps up as you get faster. Keyboard: Space is the key, . / - (or ← / →) enter a dot or dash directly, Backspace deletes. Click any letter to see and hear its code.

Inspired by a handheld Morse trainer card made by nux Modellbau.
